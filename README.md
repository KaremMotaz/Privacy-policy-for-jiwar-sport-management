# Privacy Policy — Jiwar Sport Management

**Effective date:** November 14, 2026
**App:** Jiwar Sport Management (Android package `com.jiwarsport.management`)
**Operator:** Jiwar Sports Investment ("we", "us", "our")
**Contact:** juwayryia.jiwar@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Jiwar Sport Management app ("the App") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the App on Android and iOS.

---

## 1. Who the App is for

The App is an internal management tool for **authorised staff** of clubs operated by Jiwar Sports Investment, for example management, branch managers, HR, sports, membership and security staff. It is not offered to the general public. Accounts are created and managed by the club administration. You cannot register on your own.

While doing their jobs, staff use the App to view and manage information about **club members, guests and visitors**. This policy also explains how we handle that information.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information about staff users (you)

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account and identity | Name, email address, phone number, job role, branch, staff ID, profile picture | Club administration, or you |
| Sign-in data | Login credentials, one-time verification codes (OTP), session tokens | You and our server |
| Employment and HR | HR requests (for example leave), attendance, and salary-related information shown in HR features | Club administration / HR |
| In-app activity | Records you create or approve: reports, decisions, tasks, check-ins, chat messages | You |
| Location | Your device's precise location, **only when you submit an emergency or injury report** | Your device, with your permission |
| Photos and files | Images or documents you choose to attach to reports or set as your profile picture | Your device, with your permission |

### 2.2 Information about club members, guests and visitors (processed by staff)

Depending on their role, staff can view or enter:

- **Identity and contact details:** name, national ID number, phone number, email address, home address, date of birth, photo.
- **Membership and payment details:** membership type and status, renewals, payment method and payment status. **The App does not collect or store payment card numbers.**
- **Health information:** medical conditions and injury or incident records, used for member safety and emergency response.
- **Access and security records:** guest and visitor entries, permits, QR-code check-ins, and vehicle and licence-plate information.

### 2.3 Information we do **not** collect

- We do **not** use advertising, advertising IDs or ad networks.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.
- We do **not** collect your location in the background.
- We do **not** receive your fingerprint or face data (see section 3).

---

## 3. Device permissions

The App asks for the permissions below only when you use the feature that needs them. You can refuse or withdraw any of them in your device settings. Only the related feature stops working.

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| **Location (precise and approximate)** | Attached to emergency and injury reports so responders can find the scene. Used only while the App is open and only when you submit such a report. |
| **Camera** | Scanning visitor, guest and permit QR codes, and taking photos for reports or your profile picture. Camera frames used for QR scanning are processed on your device and are not stored. |
| **Photos / files** | Attaching images or documents you select to reports, requests or your profile. |
| **Biometrics (fingerprint / Face ID)** | Confirming your identity on the device before you sign or approve sensitive decisions. Biometric checks are done entirely by your device's operating system. **We never receive or store your biometric data.** We only receive a "success" or "failure" result. |

---

## 4. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

1. Run the App's features: sign-in, role-based access, member and membership management, HR, sports and security operations, emergency response, approvals and internal chat.
2. Verify identity and keep accounts secure, for example with OTP codes and biometric confirmation.
3. Protect the safety of members, staff and visitors, including emergency and injury response.
4. Keep the records we need for running the club, and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations.
5. Provide AI-assisted features for authorised staff, such as the security AI assistant, incident and risk analysis, and dashboards. Messages and operational data used by these features are sent to and processed by our servers.
6. Troubleshoot and improve the service.

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do **not** use it for advertising or profiling.

---

## 5. Legal basis

Under Egypt's **Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020** and other applicable laws, we process personal data on the following grounds:

- it is necessary for the employment relationship with staff and for providing club membership services;
- it is needed to meet our legal obligations;
- our legitimate interests in operating and securing our clubs; and
- **consent**, where the law requires it. This includes device permissions such as location and camera, and sensitive data such as health information.

---

## 6. Where information is stored and how it is protected

- **Our servers:** App data is sent to and stored on servers operated for Jiwar Sports Investment. All communication between the App and our servers is **encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)**.
- **On your device:**
  - Sign-in tokens are stored in your device's **secure, encrypted storage** (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  - Some data, such as recent chat messages and app settings like language, is **cached locally** so the App works faster. This cached data is cleared when you sign out or uninstall the App.
- **Access control:** Each staff member can only see the data their role needs.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 7. Sharing of information

We share personal data only:

- **Within the organisation:** with authorised staff, according to their role.
- **With service providers:** such as server hosting and SMS/email providers for OTP codes. They process data only on our instructions and under confidentiality obligations.
- **For safety and emergencies:** with medical or emergency responders when needed to protect someone's life or health.
- **For legal reasons:** with authorities when the law, a court order or a government request requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the public.

We do **not** sell, rent or trade personal data.

---

## 8. Data retention

- **Staff accounts:** kept while your account or employment is active. After the account is closed, your personal data is deleted or anonymised within **90 days**, unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example, financial, employment or incident records).
- **Member, guest and visitor records:** kept while the membership or relationship is active, then deleted or anonymised within **90 days** after it ends, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
- **Data on your device:** deleted when you sign out or uninstall the App.

---

## 9. Account and data deletion

Accounts are managed by the club administration. **To delete your account and associated data:**

1. Contact your **club administration**. They can delete your account from the administration panel. **Or**
2. Email us at **juwayryia.jiwar@gmail.com** with the subject "Account Deletion Request", your full name, and the email or phone number linked to your account.

We will verify your identity and complete the request within **30 days**. Some data may be kept for the periods required by law, as described in section 8. After that it is deleted.

---

## 10. Your rights

Under applicable law, including Egypt's Law No. 151 of 2020, you have the right to:

- know what personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
- have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
- have your data deleted, subject to legal retention requirements;
- withdraw your consent at any time (for example, by turning off device permissions);
- object to or restrict certain processing; and
- file a complaint with Egypt's **Personal Data Protection Centre**.

To use any of these rights, contact your club administration or email **juwayryia.jiwar@gmail.com**.

---

## 11. Children's privacy

The App is for adult staff only and is not directed to children. Staff may process data about members under 18 as part of club membership. That data is provided by the member's parent or guardian and is handled under this policy. We do not knowingly allow children to create accounts in the App.
